About Me

Welcome to my website!

I am an independent artist who is just starting off, trying to find people who like my creations. I want to help people find their inspiration to create, imagine, and find gratitude for the little things that make our lives special.

As a small creator, I put time, care, and passion into every piece I offer. I hope you find something here you are looking for, whether it is something colorful, meaningful, or one-of-a-kind.

Thank you for visiting my site! Every purchase, share, and kind word is appreciated.

Not only are there already-made pieces, but you can also send in a photo of your own pet for a custom piece.
